Good to know

Limits

  • Settings can reset. TV and phone updates sometimes turn data sharing back on. Check again after a big update or a new device.
  • Turning things off stops new collection. It usually doesn't delete what was already shared; the reports and opt-outs above help with that.
  • Menus change. If a path no longer matches your device, search its settings for “privacy,” “viewing data” or “ads,” and let us know.
